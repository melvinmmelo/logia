## Honest framing
The head term "custom software development philippines" is owned by
established agencies (Syntactics, Symph, Exist, Arcanys, IOL…) + directories
(Clutch, TechBehemoths). A solo shop will not crack it short-term. Realistic
wins: **brand lock + local (Batangas) + niche (LMS/HRIS) + long-tail**.
